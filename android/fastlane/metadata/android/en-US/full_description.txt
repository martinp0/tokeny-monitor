Tokeny is an AI spend overview that stays on the phone. This app does not open an account and does not connect to providers.

It imports a CSV you pick in the system file picker. It reads an OpenRouter activity export and a Cursor or Grok Bot usage export. Before anything is saved, it shows a preview: how many rows it accepts, which ones it skips, and the total in dollars. Days are grouped in the Europe/Prague time zone, including daylight-saving time. Amounts stay in USD. The app does not invent a koruna exchange rate.

The saved import lives only in the app's private storage. You can delete it in the app. Uninstalling the app deletes that private storage too.

Sample data is labeled and kept apart from an import. A connected Pro budget and forecast are not here. The app has nothing to calculate them from, and it does not sell Pro.

The app does not call the internet. Its only permission, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, checks whether the phone is offline so it can label the sample. There is no analytics and no advertising.
